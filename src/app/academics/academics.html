Research & Innovation

Lukenya University boasts of a fully-fledged Directorate of Research, Innovation and Outreach in the Division of Academic and Research. The division is mandated to oversee and grow capacity and activities in R & O among faculty and students. The following are its key functions; Outreach Activities: The University supports outreach activities among faculty and students through an internal outreach fund as well as in conjunction with vaR & Ous external partners. Key engagements in this area include digital literacy outreach, public health awareness, career and life counseling, as well as the dissemination of research and innovation outputs. Linkages: Through memorandum of understanding, agreement and cooperation, the University engages industry, community, non-governmental organizations and government agencies for a variety of R & O activities such as conferences, exhibitions, outreach and research activities.
