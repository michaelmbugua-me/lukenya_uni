The Lukenya Story

Our journey began in 1991 at Lukenya hills in Mavoko, where visionary founders, the Late Honourable Rtd Justice Kasanga Mulwa and his wife Martha Kasanga Mulwa, established Lukenya Academy primary school on their cattle farm. What started as a single institution has grown into a comprehensive university dedicated to educational excellence.

Today, Lukenya University serves over 1,000 students across multiple academic disciplines, maintaining our commitment to holistic education, research, and community impact.
