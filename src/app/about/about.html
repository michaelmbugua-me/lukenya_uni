Start

1991: The story of Lukenya institutions starts in 1991 at the place known as Lukenya hills in Mavoko

University Venture

2011: The family decided to venture into university education and put together a steering committee team led by Andrew Mullei.

Construction

2012: Construction begun in 2012 with a traditional blessing prayers and ground breaking ceremony led by former Councillor Wilson Mutunga, a long-time friend of the family.

University Opens

2014: The university was officially opened by Professor Kivutha Kibwana in May and operated as Lukenya College offering diplomas and certificates in various courses.

We are Accredited

2015: The founder Justice Kasanga Mulwa passed away in March. Later that year the university was accredited.

Degree Course Admissions

2016: Admitting students in May 2016, for degree courses.

Fully Pledged University

2021: A fully-fledged university with four schools, and twenty three programmes.

We aim to be the best residential University in East Africa, by offering a wholesome learning experience for our students and faculty. We focus on creating a unique and intentional residential environment that fosters student learning and a strong sense of community. The residential model we believe ensures higher course completion rates, and lowers cost of education for the student by ensuring no commuting expenses and affordable accommodation rates.

Our Mission

To provide quality, accessible education that transforms lives, develops competent professionals, and advances knowledge through innovative teaching and research.

Our Vision

A globally competitive university recognized for academic excellence, groundbreaking research, and graduates who create positive change in their communities and the world.

Our Values

  • Excellence in all endeavors
  • Integrity and ethical conduct
  • Innovation and creativity
  • Inclusivity and diversity
