Dean of Students' Office

A Hub of Innovation and Excellence!

The Dean of students' office works to ensure students welfare in the University is catered for. The office works with other related offices to ensure students' well-being is attended to. The offices that work closely with this office include; the Guidance and Counselling office, Sports, games and clubs office, Chaplaincy office, Accommodation office as well as the welfare and career offices. The office can be reached physically anytime while on campus or in case of emergencies. The official e-mail address of the office is dos@lukenyauniversity.ac.ke .
