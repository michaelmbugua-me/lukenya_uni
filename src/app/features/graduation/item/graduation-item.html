Graduation

Class of 2025

The class of 2025 at Lukenya University was honored to be awarded by the Chief Guest H.E Former President of the Republic of Kenya Uhuru Kenyatta. The event was an outstanding moment as both parents and students celebrated as they marked their final mark in academic excellence.

Message from the Vice Chancellor, Dr. Sylvia Mueni Kasanga

On behalf of board of trustee, governing council, university senate, I officially welcome you to the 7th graduation ceremony. As we gather to celebrate the remarkable achievements of our graduands, I want to extend my heartfelt congratulations to each one of you. Today marks not only a culmination of years of hard work but also the beginning of exciting new journeys.As I reflect on where Lukenya University has come from, i am humbled of Gods goodness. it has been a period filled with inspiration and growth. I have been privileged to witness firsthand the dedication and resilience of our students and faculty, and the beauty of our university environment that nurtures innovation and collaboration.I would like to take a moment to recognize our founders, whose vision and commitment to higher education have laid the foundation for this institution. Their passion for nurturing talent and advancing knowledge is the bedrock upon which Lukenya University stands. It is our duty to honour their legacy by continuing to strive for excellence in all we do.
